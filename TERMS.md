## Copyright in what you download

Lasso downloads what you point it at. It does not decide whether you may, and
it cannot tell who owns what.

Most video and music online is protected by copyright, which belongs to the
people who made it or bought the rights, not to the site it is on or to the
person watching. Downloading a copy is copying, and whether you may do it
depends on the work and on the law where you live. In general, you are on firm
ground with:

- your own uploads and recordings;
- work in the public domain;
- work under a licence that allows copying, such as many Creative Commons
  licences (check which: some forbid commercial use or changes);
- things the owner has told you, or everyone, you may keep;
- copies your country's law specifically allows. Some countries allow a
  private copy of something you are entitled to watch; many do not, or only
  narrowly. That is yours to check, and "for personal use" is not a rule
  everywhere.

What Lasso can do is not the same as what you may do. Sharing, uploading,
selling or publicly showing a downloaded copy of someone else's work is a
separate question again, and usually needs their permission.

**Sites' own rules.** Many sites, YouTube among them, say in their terms that
you should not download except where they offer a download button. Using
Lasso does not change what you agreed with them.

**Browser cookies.** If you turn cookies on, the site sees the download as you,
signed in to your account. Anything the site's terms say about your account
applies.

**Protected content.** Lasso cannot download content protected by DRM, and
does not try to. It has no feature for getting around a paywall, a login you
do not have, or any other technical measure that controls access to a work.

The developer does not host, store or pass on anything you download, and does
not see what you download. See the [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Updates

Lasso can update itself and yt-dlp from their GitHub releases. It only
installs what matches the published checksums, and for yt-dlp only what
yt-dlp's own key has signed. Updates happen when you press the button, never
on their own.
